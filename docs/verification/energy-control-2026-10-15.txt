Implemented scope: GDD 6.2 consumer priority (high/normal/low), buffered
energy distributor with six per-face limits/modes/output priorities, passive
energy monitor. Consumer fairness rotates devices, not their input ports.
Branch priority works across separate wire components. Relay intake/output
budgets are independent, each capped by the crystalline rate. Face totals
and downstream path bottlenecks remain shared across all relevant transfers.
Network observations update at most every ten ticks and separate production,
consumption, physical moves and unmet active buffer requests. Cancelled
operation refunds do not report either production or consumption.
